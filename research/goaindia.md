# R5 report: Goa, Mandrem and India context for the Edge India Buildathon

Note: these sources date from 2025 to 2026. Many Goan news pages show today's date in the header. I used the article byline dates I verified. Where I could not verify a date, I say so.

## (a) Top local problems, with evidence

**1. Turtle nesting vs. party noise and lights (Morjim and Mandrem). This is the strongest hook in time.**
- Morjim-Mandrem nests went from 8 (2014-15) to 276 (2024-25). Hatchlings went from 331 to 12,857. In Jan-Mar 2026 Morjim had 36 nests and 3,905 eggs; Mandrem had 17 nests and 1,633 eggs. Named threats are lights, loud music, beach events and illegal shacks inside turtle protection zones. Source: [The Goan, 23 May 2026](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/turtle-nesting-boom-on-goa-beaches-despite-tourism-rush/148613.html).
- Timing: the season's first Morjim nest (99 eggs, Temwada) was reported on 28 Dec 2025, and Agonda's first was on 11 Dec. The 500 m² Temwada hatchery was reached by high-tide seawater. The campaign has run since 1997, and Morjim and Mandrem are "sensitive zones". Source: [The Goan, 28 Dec 2025](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/morjim-beach-gets-season’s-first-turtle-nesting-pit-with-99-eggs/141110.html). So nesting has **not started yet**. It starts around December. INFERRED: November, when shacks go up, is the window to prevent problems.
- Enforcement: GSPCB fined the Morjim club "Royal Tomato" Rs 21 lakh and shut it for 2 months. Its own real-time monitor logged 70 exceedances between 1 Jan and 5 Feb 2026. The club is in a Silent Zone near a nesting site. Source: [The Goan, 10 Feb 2026](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/morjim-nightclub-near-turtle-nesting-site-shut-fined-rs-21l-for-violations/143290.html).
- There were more than 900 noise complaints from Sep 2025 to Jan 2026: 750 violations caught by monitors, 50 direct complaints and 135 helpline calls. Source: [The Goan, 25 Feb 2026](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/pollution-watchdog-seeks-action-report-on-900-noise-plaints/144049.html).
- **Evidence gap.** GSPCB's own minutes say its 38 monitors "cannot identify the source of the sound" and that the data "cannot conclusively prove" that a given venue caused the exceedance. Source: [The Goan, 22 Jun 2026](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/noise-meters-come-under-pollution-watchdog-lens/149973.html). I found no public live noise dashboard. A petitioner asked the High Court for public access ([Herald](https://www.heraldgoa.in/?p=30795)), and I did not find the outcome.

**2. Land-use conversion under TCP Act Section 39A. This is the hottest civic fight.**
- More than 1,346 applications and more than 9.5 lakh m² have been reclassified. Section 17(2) was read down by the Bombay HC in March 2025. Parcem in **Pernem taluka** has some of the most re-zoned land. Source: [Mongabay, 23 Apr 2026](https://india.mongabay.com/2026/04/the-correction-clause-rewriting-goas-landscape/).
- The gazette notices are "bare… Only the survey number of the village is indicated", and about 95% of applications seek to convert orchard or natural cover to settlement. Source: [Goa Foundation PIL](https://goafoundation.org/high-court-issues-notice-on-pil-challenging-section-39a-of-tcp-act/). There is a 30-day objection window ([Better India](https://thebetterindia.com/environment/section-39a-goa-land-use-explained-11151394)).
- **Key fact for builders:** gram sabha resolutions are not binding, but objections "that name specific survey numbers or parcels of land receive closer scrutiny". The TCP Board has put some proposals in abeyance after this. Source: [The Goan, 22 Jul 2026](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/amid-sec-39a-backlash-some-proposals-kept-in-abeyance/151477.html). The "Enough is Enough" campaign gave a 15-day ultimatum in July 2026 ([The Goan](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/enough-is-enough-goa-launches-fresh-battle-against-tcp-act-gives-govt-15day-ultimatum/151240.html)).
- In Mandrem, villagers said the gram sabha should have been consulted before villa approvals. The sarpanch says 18 villas were approved in his tenure, not 90. Source: [The Goan, 20 Apr 2025](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/locals-object-to-proposed-luxury-villas-in-mandrem/129180.html). Separately, Dandoswada residents oppose a 32-flat project ([The Goan, Jul 2026](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/locals-oppose-mega-housing-projects-in-mandrem-ward/150464.html)).
- Assembly elections are due around Feb-Mar 2027 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Next_Goa_Legislative_Assembly_election)). Everything above is now politically charged.

**3. CRZ violations and coastal erosion**
- At Arambol (Girkarwada), 216 structures were found to violate CRZ and 9 were demolished under HC directives. Locals say structures owned by non-Goans along Keri-Arambol-Mandrem were "yet to" be acted on. Source: [The Goan, 6 Mar 2025](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/9-illegal-structures-demolished-at-arambol/126997.html).
- The CZMP 2019 is unlikely to go to public consultation before the elections. Goa is the only one of 11 coastal states without a finalised plan. Source: [The Goan, 13 Jun 2026](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/czmp-may-not-see-light-of-day-until-assembly-election/149561.html).
- A Deltares study named **Morjim, Mandrem** and Querim as erosion hotspots and recommended beach nourishment over seawalls ([The Goan, Oct 2025](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/coastal-study-identifies-nine-beaches-as-erosion-hotspots/137830.html); [Herald](https://www.heraldgoa.in/?p=451175)).

**4. Water.** Pernem taluka is allotted 15 MLD. Arambol gets about 3 MLD. Arambol, Morjim, Mandrem and Keri sit at the "tail point" of the supply line, and residents pay for tankers ([Herald, date not visible](https://www.heraldgoa.in/?p=178148)). Residents also link Mopa airport to drying springs ([Mongabay, 2024](https://india.mongabay.com/2024/03/residents-activists-claim-new-goa-airport-construction-has-impacted-water-availability/)).

**5. Waste.** Of Pernem's panchayats, only Morjim, Mandrem and Arambol collect wet waste. The other 17 collect dry waste only ([Herald, undated](https://www.heraldgoa.in/?p=138436)). Arambol shacks and guesthouses were warned for discharging wastewater into the sea ([Herald](https://www.heraldgoa.in/?p=349366)).

**6. Tourism shift.**
- 2025 arrivals were 1.08 crore, of which 5.18 lakh were foreign ([Goa Tourism](https://goatourism.gov.in/blog-list/goa-records-strong-and-sustained-growth-in-tourist-arrivals-2025-figures-reflect-continued-momentum/)).
- Charter flights fell from 266 to 189 between 2024 and 2025 ([The Goan](https://www.thegoan.net/editorial/﻿lost-in-the-crowd-rip-oncethriving-charter-tourism/141790.html)). Foreign arrivals are still more than 400k below 2019 ([Herald](https://www.heraldgoa.in/?p=464057)).

**7. Taxi conflict.** About a third of tourist taxis are app-based, mostly GoaMiles (about 4,000 taxis) and Goa Taxi App. The CM bars outside aggregators. The 2025 draft aggregator guidelines are contested by unions ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/1-in-3-goa-taxis-now-local-app-based-as-state-bars-outside-aggregators-125061500108_1.html); [Herald](https://www.heraldgoa.in/?p=445821)). I found no evidence the guidelines were finalised.

**8. Fisherfolk.**
- Morjim fishermen objected to a buoy-rope swim zone they were not told about. MLA Jit Arolkar promised to move it. Source: [The Goan, 16 Mar 2025](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/swimming-zone-will-be-moved-to-temb-wada-in-morjim-beach-mla/127493.html).
- Temwada fishing families want their homes protected from demolition ([The Goan, date unverified](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/protect-traditional-homes-fishing-community-to-govt/151469.html)).

**9. Konkani script politics.** Only Devanagari Konkani is official. More than 33 gram sabhas passed resolutions for equal status for Romi ([The Goan, Jun 2025](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/﻿equal-status-for-romi-konkani-a-constitutional-right-gkf/132239.html)). The CM rejected the demand in Sept 2025 ([The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/09/28/bes23-ga-language-cm.html)).

**10. Nomads and gentrification.** One Anjuna two-bedroom rent rose from Rs 16k to Rs 65k a month in about 5 years, against an average Goan income of about Rs 20k. Locals report not being hired, and shops now use "more Hindi than Konkani". Source: [Rest of World, Aug 2023](https://restofworld.org/2023/goa-india-digital-nomads-locals/).

## (b) Usable APIs and data (what can be called in 54 hours)

**Sarvam (an Edge partner).** Source: [models page](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/getting-started/models).
- **Saaras v4 speech-to-text supports Konkani (`kok-IN`).** It has transcribe, translate-to-English, translit and codemix modes. REST takes up to 30 s; batch takes up to 2 h with **diarization and timestamps**; there is also WebSocket. Source: [Saaras page](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/getting-started/models/saaras).
- **Sarvam-Translate** (23 languages) and **Sarvam Vision OCR** (23 languages, outputs JSON or Markdown) both include Konkani.
- **No Konkani** in Bulbul v3 or v4 text-to-speech, Mayura, or Sarvam-105B (the LLM).
- Credits: Rs 100 free on signup, cut from Rs 1,000 in May 2026 ([credits](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/ratelimits); [changelog](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog)). Sarvam-105B now costs Rs 29.28 / Rs 73.2 per million input/output tokens. INFERRED: ask Sarvam's Edge contact for credits.

**Open models for the Konkani gaps**
- [AI4Bharat Indic Parler-TTS](https://huggingface.co/ai4bharat/indic-parler-tts) officially lists Konkani, so it covers the text-to-speech gap.
- [IndicConformer ASR](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicConformerASR) has `kok` (MIT licence).
- [Konkani LLM paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23529) (Mar 2026): Konkani-Instruct-100k, covering Devanagari, Romi and Kannada scripts, CC BY 4.0. I could not verify download links.
- Script conversion: Konkanverter (rule-based) and Aksharamukha ([Goa University paper](https://www.ceur-ws.org/Vol-2364/13_paper.pdf)).

**Indian public digital infrastructure**
- **Bhashini**: APIs are listed on API Setu ([RS answer](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/750822/1/PQ_265_07082024_U1795_p205_p207.pdf)). I could **not verify** Konkani speech-to-text or text-to-speech.
- **UPI**: `upi://pay?pa=&pn=&am=&tn=` deep links and QR codes need no onboarding ([linking spec v1.6 mirror](https://www.labnol.org/files/linking.pdf)). This is the only public-infrastructure integration that is trivially doable in 54 h.
- **ONDC**: needs whitelisting, and pre-production access needs a demo and approval ([ONDC GitHub](https://github.com/ONDC-Official)). Not feasible.
- **Account Aggregator**: sandbox access goes through a form and Sahamati validation ([Sahamati](https://developer.sahamati.org.in/sahamatinet-mvp/integration-steps/sandbox-onboarding)). Not feasible.
- **DigiLocker**: onboarding not verified.
- **IndiaAI AIKosh**: 7,541 datasets and 273 models as of Feb 2026, sign-in required ([PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/feb/doc2026210784401.pdf)).

**Goa data**
- Tourism arrivals: the Goa Tourism blog and Assembly answer PDFs ([example](https://static.goavidhansabha.gov.in/goalpub/docs/question_docs/file_6490b0d3-6a49-4a96-8e50-fcc919a821e1.pdf)).

## (c) Host-community ethics pitfalls

**Edge's own stated norms**
- "We're guests in Mandrem and Goa. Engage respectfully with local customs and support neighborhood businesses" ([india26](https://www.edgecity.live/india26)).
- Ground Floor's stated method is to "begin with problems owners name, build inside their work, and publish what changed". It costs the owner nothing, and the route is "Healdsburg → Mandrem" ([groundfloorlabs.org](https://groundfloorlabs.org)).

**The critique judges will know**
- Próspera was ruled unconstitutional by Honduras' Supreme Court and has been called "privatised imperialism" ([Dezeen, Dec 2025](https://www.dezeen.com/2025/12/18/infrastructure-prospera-honduras-sam-venis/amp/)). It faces an approximately $10.8B claim, a figure from an advocacy source ([IRR, Oct 2025](https://www.irreview.org/articles/2025/10/23/prspera-honduras-a-controversial-experiment-in-corporate-governance)).
- A 2025 Berlin roundtable was titled "Network States or Neo-colonialism" ([FTC Berlin](https://ftcberlin2025.sched.com/2025-06-11/list/descriptions/type/Roundtable/Breadcon)). Buterin's concern is that network states become "clubs for the rich" ([ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/news/the-srinivasan-buterin-democratic-republic)).
- I found no published local criticism of Edge City India itself. The Savlar corpus has no coverage of this topic.

**Pitfalls to avoid** (INFERRED synthesis)
1. **Building for Goans without Goans.** Show a named local user or co-builder on video, with consent. Ground Floor is the natural bridge.
2. **Taking sides 3-4 months before the election** on 39A, taxis or Romi. Build citation-first tools that are neutral about who is right, and leave positions to people.
3. **Enforcement tech aimed only at small local operators.** Locals already complain that bigger non-Goan structures go untouched (Arambol article).
4. **Publishing turtle nest locations.** Exact coordinates should never be public.
5. **Faking Konkani** with Marathi or Hindi voices, or picking one script. Support both Devanagari and Romi.
6. **Agents speaking for locals.** This repeats Agent Village V1's failure modes of fabrication and false attribution. Keep a human yes on anything that is published or filed.
7. **Leaving with nothing behind on 1 Nov.** Hand off to a panchayat, NGO or Ground Floor under an open-source licence.

## (d) Idea seeds (all framing choices are INFERRED)

**1. Sabha: a Konkani-first gram sabha memory**
- How it works: Saaras v4 batch transcription (Konkani, codemix, diarization) produces minutes in Devanagari, Romi and English. Every resolution, objection and promise is linked to its timestamp, which reuses the Savlar claim-to-moment and "who changed their mind" design. A human approval gate comes before publishing, following the Agent Village pattern.
- Who cares: Mandrem villagers who said they were not consulted on villa approvals; panchayats that pass 39A resolutions.
- Needs: in Goa, with consent from one real meeting or a role-played one.

**2. Survey Number Sentinel (39A and CRZ watch)**
- How it works: Sarvam Vision OCR reads gazette PDFs. The tool extracts survey numbers, maps them to village and ward, and sends a Konkani or English alert inside the 30-day window. It then helps a villager draft a *parcel-specific* objection, the kind that gets "closer scrutiny". A human signs; nothing is filed automatically.
- Who cares: gram sabhas, Goa Foundation, Parcem (Pernem).
- Needs: can be built remotely; it has the strongest data hook.
- Risk: it is political, so keep it strictly informational.

**3. Dark Beach Pact (before turtle season)**
- How it works: shacks and venues near Morjim and Mandrem nesting zones opt in to a lights-and-quiet protocol. Volunteers log disturbances with phone dB readings plus hashed, timestamped evidence. This reuses the sealed-evidence design from arcminute and fermata, and directly addresses GSPCB's admitted "cannot identify the source" gap.
- Who cares: the Forest Department team at Temwada, shack owners who want legitimacy, GSPCB, the Mandrem MLA.
- Needs: in Goa.
- Rule: never publish nest locations.

**4. Guest Ledger: did Edge's money stay in Mandrem?**
- How it works: residents and their agents pay local businesses through UPI deep links and opt in to aggregate logging. A public dashboard shows rupees spent with Goan-owned versus outside businesses. Agent Village agents prefer local vendors, with a human approval gate.
- Who cares: Edge organisers (Timour), local restaurants and Ground Floor.
- Why it matters: it is a direct, measurable answer to the popup-city critique.
- Needs: in Goa, plus resident opt-in.
- Privacy: publish aggregates only.

**5. Shack-to-world voice concierge (for Ground Floor)**
- How it works: a homestay or shack owner speaks Konkani; Saaras translates to English; listings and replies are produced; Indic Parler-TTS speaks back in Konkani. It checks that the homestay registration number is displayed. This reuses ns-steward's voice-first design.
- Who cares: Ground Floor's business owners.
- Needs: in Goa.

**6. Tail-End Water Log**
- How it works: residents report tap and tanker supply by Konkani voice note. The tool builds a ward-level ledger and compares it against new-project approvals.
- Who cares: Arambol and Mandrem households, PWD, and the MLA, who reportedly asked for a construction halt pending the 30 MLD scheme ([Herald](https://www.heraldgoa.in/?p=14020)).
- Needs: it can be built remotely, but the demo would use seeded data and must say so.

**7. Two-Script Reader**
- How it works: OCR of Devanagari-only government notices, then Romi transliteration and plain-language Konkani or English summaries. It is framed as accessibility, not as a position on official status.
- Who cares: Romi readers, the tiatr community and Goa University.
- Needs: can be built remotely.

My pick for "judges cannot ignore it" (INFERRED): **#1 combined with #2**, a citation-first civic memory for villages. It directly reuses Savlar, batch Konkani speech-to-text and human approval gates. It fits Ground Floor and Week 3's governance theme, and it answers the "extractive popup" critique by leaving a working tool with a panchayat. #4 is the strongest Edge-specific ethics play if the user is in Goa.
