# R7: What the popup-city movement says is still unsolved, its philosophy, and where a build could hit it

**Savlar coverage.** The corpus covers only four AI podcasts: Dwarkesh (13 of 143 episodes processed), Latent Space (18 of 286), MLST (7 of 272) and No Priors (6 of 185). Searches for "popup cities / network states / Zuzalu", "d/acc", "public goods funding" and "institutional memory" all returned "the corpus may not cover this question". Only the AI-agent, human-agency and trust quotes below come from it.

**Not found.** I found no public trace of an "NYU Ethical Intelligence Lab" on the web. The phrase "the best way to build the future is to live in it" does not appear in any Edge City source I read; the closest wording is quoted in (b).

## (a) The movement's top 8 open problems

**1. Knowledge evaporates when a village ends ("what happens next?")**
- Vitalik on Zuzalu: "the experiment left unanswered a key question: what happens next?" ([societies](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/12/17/societies.html))
- Edge City Lanna recap: "There is so much that happens at our villages that we find out about in retrospect." ([Lanna recap](https://edgecitylanna.substack.com/p/edge-city-lanna-recap-whats-next))
- Edge City's own roadmap, step 5, still only says: "A shared archive of experiments could track what's been tested, what needs refinement, and what could be built next." It also says "Most projects begin in one village and continue in the next." ([roadmap](https://www.edgecity.live/roadmap)). INFERRED: the word "could" suggests the archive is not built yet.
- In Dec 2025 Edge City hired Katherine Jones "to focus on ecosystem building: tracking what emerges from our gatherings" ([2025 letter](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/edge-city-2025-community-letter)).
- The Request for Experiments says: "A good experiment leaves something behind: a writeup, a dataset, a protocol, an open-source tool, a failed attempt other people can learn from…" It also lists "Portable identity across village apps" as a wanted experiment ([RFE](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/edge-citys-request-for-experiments)).
- From a third party, quoting the ZuCity founder Kiba: "Kiba likes to say Zuzalu doesn't exist… there's no single place, channel or event today through which you could reach that same community or capture the ethos and magic of that event." ([P2P wiki](https://wiki.p2pfoundation.net/ZuCity_Japan_and_the_Evolution_of_Pop-Ups), from [X](https://x.com/AnalogueUSB/status/2111635871443374169))
- The next village is Bhutan, Nov 11-19 (task context), so continuity is a live need this month.

**2. Showing impact: moving from "container" to "catalyst"**
- Timour's 2025 letter is titled "From Container to Catalyst". It says: "We're now shifting our attention to what actually gets built inside these environments; the incubator side of society incubator."
- On serendipity: "Serendipity keeps being off-the-charts, and we don't fully understand why." ([2025 letter](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/edge-city-2025-community-letter))
- The RFE asks for "results other people can learn from" and names the loop "hypothesis, prototype, real-world use, public learning" ([RFE](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/edge-citys-request-for-experiments)).

**3. Helpful individual agents do not add up to a healthy collective**
The Agent Village V1 write-up (Kosters, Law and Vendrov, Jul 31 2026) says:
- "Agents hallucinated personal details, attributed invented ideas to their principals, exhausted shared credits, and frustrated some attendees. Individual alignment did not automatically produce collective alignment."
- "Agents can make it easier to generate possible activities than for communities to absorb them, creating a need to govern shared resources and attention."
- "Simocracy showed a process of delegated voting without principal ratification."
- Index Network's own lesson: "opportunity abundance will need to be solved."
- Open questions they list: Representation; Autonomy and control ("understand, correct, withdraw, or ratify"); Collective alignment; Human outcomes. Timour wants to try "a layered set of mezzanine agents" that sit between personal agents and the collective.

All from [Cosmos](https://blog.cosmos-institute.org/p/we-gave-a-village-personal-ai-agents).

The pre-registered predictions for the experiment include "some agents will go awol" and "Humans will delegate operations faster than they delegate relationships" ([EE26 agent post](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/the-agent-village-experiment-at-edge-esmeralda-2026)).

**4. Governance and legitimacy beyond founder control**
- Vitalik, 2025: governance is in "a 'trough of disillusionment', as we have realized the downfalls of formalizing governance too early". He thinks AI and ZK "could solve their two biggest problems: attention overload and fatigue, and collapse into social games where people vote based on how their vote will be perceived." He also notes "'forking as governance' works perfectly fine" for short popups, and that Vitalia "split into two forks" ([societies](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/12/17/societies.html)).
- Vitalik, 2022: "the problem of how to settle into something other than founder control is important… a roadmap toward wider participation in governance is not [baked in]. It should be." ([network states](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2022/07/13/networkstates.html))
- Edge City lists "Advance Governance and Community Models" as one of its three impact areas ([about](https://www.edgecity.live/about)).

**5. Coordination overload and onboarding**
- Timour: "when people arrived on week 4, they were excited to be there, but felt like the vibe had already been set and weren't sure how to enter it."
- Also Timour: "if everyone does a session, then the calendar can get overwhelming." ([Supernuclear case study](https://supernuclear.substack.com/cp/161397355))
- Vitalik's ideal: "a college at 25% intensity… Many popups I've been to were too much on the side of tiring me out." ([societies](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/12/17/societies.html))
- Edge City India is three weeks with rolling weekly themes ([programming preview](https://edgecityindia2026.substack.com/p/programming-preview-for-edge-city)). INFERRED: late arrivals are a structural feature here, not an edge case.

**6. Real local integration versus extraction**
- Vitalik: "Involving locals in a non-superficial way is hard… effective local community building requires coming back to a place for years, and not just doing a one-off." ([societies](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/12/17/societies.html))
- Edge City's framing is "Integration, not exit" ([society incubator](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/part-1-building-a-society-incubator)), and its India norm is "Honor Our Host Community" ([india26](https://www.edgecity.live/india26)).
- Ground Floor (Goa) asks: "What would it take to close that gap for Konkani? Who should own the data? Who benefits when the infrastructure is built?" and "how communities remain authors of their own future." On their Healdsburg pilot: "We expected enthusiasm. What we found was discernment." ([Ground Floor](https://edgecityindia2026.substack.com/p/the-future-arrives-locally))
- A Patagonia fellow, Maxwell Opondo: "Solid plan. Wrong problem." ([guest post](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/wrong-problem-right-community))

**7. Funding public goods and the village itself**
- Vitalik: "Popups are expensive… Edge City is not cheap to attend." Also: "Culture does not have the same profit motive." ([societies](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/12/17/societies.html))
- Edge City: "finding the right financial model to make them sustainable… is an ongoing experiment" ([society incubator](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/part-1-building-a-society-incubator)).
- Also Edge City: "Grants are slow. Venture capital often requires a company when what's needed is a movement, a tool, or a new kind of public good." ([Long.xyz post](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/a-new-way-to-back-bold-longterm-projects))
- SHIFT d/acc grants deployed "$400k to builders working on privacy, information resilience, and decentralized infrastructure" ([2025 letter](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/edge-city-2025-community-letter)). The tracks are biosecurity, cyber defense, cryptography, information resilience, neurotech and social governance ([grants](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/grantsandfellowships)).
- The Project Odin workshop on Oct 30 is about sustainable OSS funding (task context).

**8. Regression to the mean, and the fragility of permanent nodes**
- Vitalik: "over time, popups would get shorter in duration, smaller in scope, and more generic in substance… I always fear the 'regression to the mean' that they will turn into glorified coworkingspaces." ([societies](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/12/17/societies.html))
- Kiba: "most pop-ups are experiential tourism… Then everyone goes home." ([P2P wiki](https://wiki.p2pfoundation.net/ZuCity_Japan_and_the_Evolution_of_Pop-Ups))
- Vitalik gave about $500k in ETH to Zuitzerland as a "long-term Zuzalu hub". That framing is the project's own ([ns.com](https://ns.com/dashboard/zuitzerland), [bitcoinist](https://bitcoinist.com/ethereum-founder-buterin-funds-swiss-project-with-274-eth-whats-the-agenda/)).
- Network School's Malaysian licence was reportedly revoked in July 2026 ([secondary guide only](https://malaysia4u.com/network-school-guide); not verified).

## (b) Philosophical frames the judges will respond to

- **Society incubator and "micro-exits".** Edge City's own words: "The systems that shape our time… were made for a prior world." The goal is to "temporarily create a bubble of experimentation… and then merge the learnings and tools we develop back into the mainstream." ([about](https://www.edgecity.live/about), [society incubator](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/part-1-building-a-society-incubator))
- **Living in the future (closest match to the requested phrase).** "experiment with living in the future they want to see" ([society incubator](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/part-1-building-a-society-incubator)), and residents came "to prototype the future they want to live in, together" ([EE26 review](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/edge-esmeralda-2026-month-in-review)).
- **d/acc.** "Build technologies that shift the offense/defense balance toward defense… not rely on handing over more power to centralized authorities." The goal is "a world where we preserve human agency." Crypto communities are "appealing incubators and testbeds for d/acc technologies that fundamentally work on groups rather than individuals… Crypto people just do things, together." ([d/acc one year later](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/01/05/dacc2.html))
- **AI as the engine, humans as the steering wheel.** Humans give "a few hundred bits, but each of those bits is a well-considered and very high-quality bit", and this works "without enshrining a single AI at the center" ([aihumans](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/02/28/aihumans.html)).
- **Pluralism and balance of power.** "you are not allowed to be hegemonic, but you are encouraged to be impactful, and to empower others" ([balance of power](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/12/30/balance_of_power.html)). Also "Let a thousand societies bloom" and its archipelago section ([societies](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/12/17/societies.html)).
- **Human autonomy under AI (Cosmos, the research partner).** "Agents may increase democratic bandwidth while weakening the formative experience of participation." Also: "The measure of success is whether agents enable us to live more fulfilling, human lives." ([Cosmos](https://blog.cosmos-institute.org/p/we-gave-a-village-personal-ai-agents)) Cosmos is promoting the book *Free Agents*, "on retaining human autonomy under frontier AI" ([EE26 review](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/edge-esmeralda-2026-month-in-review)).
- **Emergence and the bazaar.** "we expect the best ideas… to emerge bottoms-up from the community." Also: "Community becomes the first validator." ([society incubator](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/part-1-building-a-society-incubator), [2025 letter](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/edge-city-2025-community-letter))
- **Being wrong in public.** "Honest predictions, written down before the village starts, so we can be wrong in public." ([EE26 agent post](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/the-agent-village-experiment-at-edge-esmeralda-2026)) INFERRED: this matches arcminute's sealed predictions almost exactly.
- **Open-source everything.** "we'll iterate and open source everything we learn" ([roadmap](https://www.edgecity.live/roadmap)).
- **Wise city, not smart city.** A Gelephu planner: "We're not just building a smart city. We're building a wise city." ([Bhutan recap](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/bhutan-and-the-blueprint-for-a-wise-city)) This bridges to Bhutan (Nov 11-19).

## (d) Five idea seeds (all INFERRED designs)

**1. "Afterlife": the Shared Archive of Experiments (Roadmap step 5, built)**
- **Problems it targets:** 1, 2 and 8.
- **What it does:** an agent-built, open archive of every Edge India experiment and session: hypothesis, protocol, result, artifacts, open questions and who is continuing it. Each claim links to the exact moment in a talk recording or note.
- **Honest scoring:** predictions are sealed before an experiment and revealed after it.
- **Handoff:** the archive exports a "carry-forward" bundle for Edge City Bhutan.
- **Reuse:** Savlar's claim-to-moment pipeline, arcminute's SHA-256 sealing, and an agentvillage mini-app or skill, so personal agents can answer "what was tried on X before?"
- **Pitch line:** it turns "we find out about in retrospect" into a record you can query.
- **Logistics:** works solo and remote. It is stronger with in-person recording of the Oct 28-30 sessions.

**2. "Ratify": provenance and principal-ratification for Agent Village**
- **Problems it targets:** 3 and 4, and the V1 failure modes directly.
- **Provenance tags:** every statement an agent makes about its human carries a tag: *said by human (with link)*, *inferred*, or *unsupported*. Unsupported attributions are blocked.
- **Consequential actions:** fund votes and RSVPs on someone's behalf need a signed human "yes". This extends the av-approval plugin (task context).
- **Red-teaming:** a "paper twin" of the EdgeOS and Index MCP servers (from dress-rehearsal) replays the V1 failure modes against agents before go-live, with independent verifiers (from harness-lab).
- **Fit:** it matches the NYU lab's partner role and Cosmos's question about whether people can "understand, correct, withdraw, or ratify".
- **Logistics:** works solo and remote, with a repo, a demo video and a failure-mode scoreboard.

**3. "Mezzanine": a commons steward for attention and shared credits**
- **Problems it targets:** 3 and 5.
- **What it does:** a group-level agent that budgets shared credits and village attention. Personal agents bid for scarce slots (venues, intro bandwidth, calendar density) under caps or quadratic weights.
- **Privacy:** reservation values stay private through batna-protocol-style encrypted matching.
- **Late arrivals:** a "week-4 arrival" onboarding brief, generated from the archive in seed 1.
- **Fit:** it builds the architecture Timour named ("mezzanine agents") and Index's "opportunity abundance" problem.
- **Logistics:** works solo. It needs EDGEOS_API_KEY and Index access, which are unconfirmed.

**4. "Ground Truth": a Goan voice field log for Ground Floor**
- **Problems it targets:** 6 and 1.
- **What it does:** a voice-first tool (based on ns-steward; Sarvam and ElevenLabs are village partners) in which local business owners record needs, decisions and what they chose to ignore. The record stays with them and is consent-gated. It is matched to resident skills.
- **Why it fits:** it answers "Who should own the data?" and Ground Floor's finding about "discernment".
- **Logistics:** needs to be in Goa, with Ground Floor as a partner. Konkani support is UNVERIFIED and is a real risk. This seed is the most "community-help" in spirit, and its risk of overstepping the "Honor Our Host Community" norm is high if it is done without local co-design.

**5. "Proof of Experiment": pay-on-proof micro-grants for village public goods**
- **Problems it targets:** 7 and 2.
- **What it does:** residents pledge small funds to experiments. Release depends on verifiable evidence (zkTLS proofs of a deployed repo, dataset or published writeup), with fermata-style escrow.
- **Steering:** humans set the criteria and AI checks the evidence ("engine / steering wheel").
- **Fit:** it ties into the Project Odin OSS-funding workshop on Oct 30 and SHIFT's info-resilience track.
- **Logistics:** works solo and remote. The crypto angle fits Week 3, but there is a risk of looking like Hacker House bounty material instead of a community build.

**Strongest combination (INFERRED):** seeds 1 and 2 as one build. "Afterlife" is the memory layer and "Ratify" is the honesty layer for the village's agents. Together they answer the two problems the organizers wrote down themselves: the "shared archive of experiments" on their roadmap and the V1 agent failure modes. They also reuse most of the user's existing work.
