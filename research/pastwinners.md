## (b) Patterns

1. **Village-native builds get amplified.** Builds that residents used during the month and that produced a number get the spotlight: Cursive's dashboard, Fulcra, the solar datacenter, the food database (86% of 300+ foods), Simocracy's $10k portfolio, and Agent Village's 147 accepted conversations ([Cosmos](https://blog.cosmos-institute.org/p/we-gave-a-village-personal-ai-agents)). Two of them became next year's Request for Experiments ([RFE](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/edge-citys-request-for-experiments)).
2. **Honest scope disclosure is a standing rule.** Both Esmeralda 2024 and Lanna required entrants to state how long they had been building. Prior work was allowed. This suits the user's style of honest documentation (INFERRED).
3. **Tech and nontech both count.** Lanna's criterion said "tech or non tech", a film was featured, and LongX won 2nd with an education presentation. Lanna also had community-vote and "Most Creative" prizes.
4. **Judges are founders, VCs and Edge's own team.** Notion, NFX, Long Journey and 1517 judged Esmeralda; Vitalik, Timour, Janine and Telamon were on Lanna's list of "Speakers and Judges", which doesn't separate the two roles ([Lanna page](https://www.edgecity.live/lanna-hackathon)). INFERRED: they reward a big vision for human flourishing combined with something that visibly works.
5. **This rubric was shaped with NYU's Ethical Intelligence Lab.** That lab is an Agent Village partner ([agents.edgecity.live](https://agents.edgecity.live/)) and is running an agentic participatory-budgeting experiment in Goa ([repo](https://github.com/The-Cybernetic-Society/edge-city-nyu)). INFERRED: expect weight on human agency, consent, representation fidelity and harm avoidance.
6. **Field is small.** 2 teams were registered as of today ([buildathon page](https://build.edgecity.live/events/india-buildathon)). The top 3 demo live at Demo Day, so the build has to survive a live demo.

## (e) Gaps nobody seems to cover

1. **An independent check on agent fidelity.** V1's failure modes were invented facts about users, ideas credited to people who never had them, and funds allocated with no final human yes ([agents site](https://agents.edgecity.live/), [Cosmos](https://blog.cosmos-institute.org/p/we-gave-a-village-personal-ai-agents)). Current PRs add apps and approval cards, but none checks whether agents' preference predictions ([#335](https://github.com/Edge-City/agentvillage/pull/335)) match how residents actually vote in the treasury ballot (H2 in [#193](https://github.com/Edge-City/agentvillage/pull/193)).
   - INFERRED fit: arcminute's sealed-then-revealed predictions, plus harness-lab-style scoring.
2. **Private intention and conditional-commitment layer.** Cosmos explicitly asks for this ("I'll host if six others join"), and I saw no PR for it.
   - INFERRED fit: batna-protocol.
3. **Pay-on-proof for village and open-source work.** The Project Odin funding workshop is Oct 30 ([india26](https://www.edgecity.live/india26)), but no agent-payment-on-verified-work build is visible.
   - INFERRED fit: fermata.
4. **A memory of the village's talks.** Village Digest covers Telegram and Edge Library covers pasted text. Nobody visibly maps three weeks of talks for agreement, disagreement and changes of mind.
   - The build would have to set itself clearly apart from Edge Library.
5. **Tools for Goan hosts with measured before/after results.** Ground Floor has published no outcomes yet. INFERRED gaps: local-language voice using Sarvam or ElevenLabs (both partners), and the "respect the host community" norm.
6. **Governing shared agent resources.** Credit exhaustion and social overload were V1 failures, and the only control I saw is the quota in Gold Stars.
