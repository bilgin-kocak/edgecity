# Edge India Buildathon: research and ideas

Research and project ideas for the [Edge India Buildathon](https://build.edgecity.live/events/india-buildathon). It runs in Mandrem, Goa, or remotely, from Wed 28 Oct 2026 11:30 IST until submissions close on Fri 30 Oct 18:00 IST. Demo Day and the awards are on Sat 31 Oct. The prizes are $3,000 cash plus two Edge City tickets, $7,000 combined.

**Start with [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md).** It is the full report: the recommendation, the strategic read on judging, the six finalists with build plans, your first 6 hours, and sources.

## Bottom line

**The jury is already known in one important way.** The judging criteria were explored with NYU's Ethical Intelligence Lab. That lab is also a research partner in Agent Village V2, the village's live experiment giving residents personal AI agents, and its MoralMod system was deployed there this month. Agent Village's own V1 write-up names the failures it cares most about: agents fabricating things about their people and misattributing ideas to them. The project the jury is least likely to ignore measures those failures, honestly and with consent, against rules the organizers wrote themselves.

**Which idea to pick depends on where you are:**

| Your situation | Pick |
|---|---|
| In Goa or with an in-village teammate, with written OK from the Agent Village team and NYU by Wed 14:00 | **Said-By**: residents grade what their own agent says about them, against the platform's own rules |
| In Goa or with a teammate, but those approvals don't come | **Both Seats**: residents judge agent actions from both sides, the person acting and the person affected |
| Remote and solo | **Who Was Studied?**: a "who was in the trials" label for health claims, plus a sealed test of whether AI assistants disclose it |
| In Goa and you want the biggest room moment | **Go Dark**: a phone game for the whole Demo Day room about Edge's own beach lights and turtle hatchlings |
| In Goa with a musician | **Chorus, with receipts**: a song made only of residents' own consented words |

## How this was made

This was a multi-agent research run on 27 Oct 2026:

1. **Research.** Seven parallel researchers covered the Agent Village repo and V1 write-up, the NYU lab and likely judges, past Edge City winners, the Edge City India Substack and wiki, Goa and Indian digital public infrastructure, frontier tooling, and the popup-city movement.
2. **Ideation.** Six ideators each wrote five ideas, from these angles: village infrastructure, host community, art and experience, ethics and governance, body and mind, and contrarian.
3. **Curation.** A curator merged the 30 ideas into 15.
4. **Judging.** Three judges scored every idea, through the lenses of the Edge City jury, feasibility, and demo-day crowd.
5. **Stress test.** A red team tried to kill each of the six finalists, then wrote a battle plan.
6. **Synthesis.** A draft, a fact-check critic and a revision produced the final report.

Claims carry source links, and inferences are labelled INFERRED. **The rubric was not published when this was written,** so re-check the buildathon page on Wednesday morning.
